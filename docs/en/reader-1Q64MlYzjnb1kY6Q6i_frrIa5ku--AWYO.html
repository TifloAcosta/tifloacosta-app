All TifloAcosta materials, finally in one place where they won't get lost

Hello hello, lovely people.

For a long time, whenever I shared a guide, a course, a document or any other TifloAcosta resource, I did it through WhatsApp.

The problem usually came afterwards: you had to save the file, remember where you had put it and, when you needed it again, begin an archaeological expedition through chats, downloads and folders.

And then came the classic message:

“Tony, could you send me that thing you shared a while ago again?”

That is exactly what I want to avoid from now on. We now have a permanent place where the shared TifloAcosta materials can always be found.

The idea is simple: WhatsApp will still be used to let you know when something new is available, but the materials themselves will always be kept in one place, which will continue to be updated with new guides, courses, documents and other resources.

Access the shared TifloAcosta materials

When you open the page, you will find two options: materials in Spanish or resources in English. Choose the language you want and the corresponding Google Drive folder will open.

No registration on the page, no forms and no seven-screen obstacle course just to get where we wanted to go in the first place.

Save the access once and, whenever you need something, simply go back there. Much easier than hoping that WhatsApp file from three months ago is still hiding somewhere on your phone.

And now the important bit: keeping the folder close at hand

Opening it from the TifloAcosta page works perfectly well, but we can make things even easier.

Once you have chosen Spanish or English and opened the corresponding Google Drive folder, you can add a shortcut to it inside your own Drive.

To put it less technically, it is like keeping the door to the folder right inside your own house.

You are not copying all the materials or creating a separate folder. The shortcut simply takes you back to the original TifloAcosta folder.

And this is where it becomes really useful: whenever I add a new guide, course, document or other resource, you will not need to do anything. The next time you open the shortcut, the new material will already be there.

No more searching through old WhatsApp messages, no more wondering where on earth that file was saved, and no more placing your faith in a folder called “Downloads”, where some files go in and then apparently disappear into another dimension.

Once the shortcut has been created, the folder will be available from your own Google Drive and you can return to it whenever you want.

What you can and cannot do

The folders are shared as read-only. You can open and read the documents and, whenever you want, download a file or save a personal copy.

You cannot edit, move, cut or delete the original TifloAcosta files or change the contents of the shared folders.

If you ever remove the shortcut you created from your Google Drive, only that shortcut disappears from your account. The original folder and all its documents remain intact.

If you make a personal copy of a document, remember that your copy becomes independent. If I update the original later, your personal copy will not update automatically. To make sure you are always reading the latest version, open the document from the shared folder.

From Windows or Mac, using a web browser

You need to be signed in to your Google Account to create the shortcut. There are two ways to reach the same result.

Option A: directly from the open folder

  1. Open the TifloAcosta page, choose Spanish or English and open the corresponding folder.
  2. Make sure you are signed in to your Google Account.
  3. Near the top of the page, look for the control that lets you add a shortcut to Drive. With a screen reader, it may be announced as Add shortcut to Drive or something very similar. Activate it.
  4. Choose My Drive, or a specific folder inside your Drive, as the location for the shortcut.
  5. Confirm by selecting Add.

Option B: from Shared with me

Use this option if you cannot find the shortcut control above.

  1. Open Google Drive with your account and choose Shared with me from the main navigation.
  2. Find the TifloAcosta folder and open its options. With a mouse, you can right-click it. With JAWS or NVDA, place focus on the folder and use its actions menu, context menu or the corresponding More options control.
  3. Choose Organize, then Add shortcut.
  4. Choose My Drive, or a specific folder inside your Drive.
  5. Confirm with Add shortcut or Add, depending on what the interface displays.

Both methods give you the same result. From that point on, you can open the TifloAcosta folder from My Drive without having to find the original link again.

If you use Google Drive for desktop

If Google Drive for desktop is installed on your Windows PC or Mac, the shortcut you added to My Drive becomes part of your Google Drive structure.

Depending on your configuration, you can then reach it from File Explorer in Windows or Finder on Mac. Remember that you are still opening a read-only shared folder: you can open and copy the files, but you cannot modify the originals.

From iPhone or iPad with VoiceOver

  1. Open the TifloAcosta page, choose Spanish or English and open the corresponding folder. Make sure the Google Drive app is installed and you are signed in to your account.
  2. In the Google Drive app, open Shared and locate the TifloAcosta folder.
  3. With VoiceOver, move through the controls until you find the More button for that folder and activate it with a double-tap.
  4. Choose Add shortcut to Drive.
  5. Select My Drive, or the folder where you want to place the shortcut.
  6. Tap Add.

If you prefer to use the browser on your iPhone or iPad instead of the app, follow the browser procedure above after signing in to Google Drive.

From Android with TalkBack

  1. Open the TifloAcosta page, choose Spanish or English and open the corresponding folder. Make sure the Google Drive app is installed and you are signed in to your account.
  2. In the Google Drive app, open Shared and locate the TifloAcosta folder.
  3. With TalkBack, place focus on the folder and open its corresponding More control.
  4. Choose Add shortcut to Drive.
  5. Select My Drive, or the folder where you want to place the shortcut.
  6. Confirm with Add.

If you prefer to use the mobile browser instead of the app, follow the browser procedure above after signing in to Google Drive.

One doorway to everything new

The idea is for this system to become the main reference point for TifloAcosta materials. Create the shortcut once, and from then on you will always know where to look whenever you want to check for something new or recover a document you can no longer find.

There is nothing to synchronize manually and no need to add the folder again. The shortcut always points to the same place, so whenever I add an article, guide, course or another document, it will be there the next time you visit.

And if a file you previously saved in WhatsApp or on your device gets lost, deleted, or decides to move out without leaving a forwarding address, you will not need to ask me for it again. Just open the folder, find the document and read or download it again.

You will need an Internet connection to access files that are not already saved on your device.

No searching through old messages, no endless collection of separate files and, above all, no more: “Tony, where was that document you sent ages ago?”
