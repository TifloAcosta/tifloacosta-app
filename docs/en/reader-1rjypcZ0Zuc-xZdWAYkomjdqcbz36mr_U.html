AudioGameAccess: games to enjoy by ear, on your own or with others

24 October 2026 · TifloAcosta

Hello, hello, lovely people.

Today I’m bringing you something for those of us who enjoy audio games. It’s called AudioGameAccess, and the idea is simple: to let blind people enjoy games of skill, cards, strategy and words without depending on what is shown on the screen. Here, hearing isn’t a second-class guest; it does much of the work.

What is AudioGameAccess?

It is a free audio gaming platform created by Mathieu Martin for blind and low-vision players. It is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows computers. Rather than one game, it offers a collection of games played through sounds, spoken prompts and familiar screen readers.

How do you play without seeing the screen?

On iPhone and iPad, it works with VoiceOver. Some games turn almost the entire screen into a gesture surface, allowing you to swipe, tap or double-tap to play instead of having to hunt for buttons. There is also a visible-controls mode, useful for people with some remaining sight or those who want to play with others.

On Mac, you use VoiceOver and the keyboard. On Windows, the platform works with JAWS or NVDA, also using the keyboard. Sound effects help you work out where things are and what is happening; with headphones, some sounds are positioned in space, as though coming from different directions around you. Large text and high contrast are available for people with some usable vision.

What can you play?

There is plenty of variety. Archery and bowling test your aim; Feather Flight features a chicken trying to escape as far as possible; and card games include Crazy Eights, President, Golf, Ninety-nine and Uno. There are also dice games, Hangman, Sea Battle, word games, Parcheesi and Metropolis, where you become a mayor and build a city. The computer versions also feature games such as blackjack, Texas Hold’em poker and bingo.

The collection may change with updates. Each game has instructions, although not every manual or piece of content is translated into every supported language.

Can you play with other people?

Yes. To get started without any complications, you can play offline, on your own or against computer-controlled opponents, without creating an account. If you want to play over the Internet, you can set up a table, invite friends and share a game with people using other compatible devices.

Online play includes text and voice chat, private messages, friends lists and leaderboards. Certain games can also be saved and continued later. To play online you need a free account: enter an email address, confirm it with a code, and choose a password and player name.

Languages, price and compatibility

The platform is free and its interface is available in Spanish, English, French, Italian and Portuguese. The developer lists iOS or iPadOS 17 or later for iPhone and iPad, macOS 14 Sonoma or later for Mac, and Windows 10 or 11 for PCs. At present, the official website does not list an Android app.

Official download and installation links

iPhone and iPad: install AudioGameAccess from the App Store

Windows 10 and 11: visit the official website and select “Download for Windows”

Mac: visit the official website and select “Download for Mac”

On Mac, the developer says to open the downloaded ZIP file and drag AudioGameAccess into the Applications folder. On Windows and Mac, the application updates when launched; on iPhone and iPad, updates are distributed through the App Store.

This is not a promise that everybody will like every game, or that bugs will never appear. It is simply another option worth trying. And it has something in its favour: it lets blind people join in as players, rather than spectators who need somebody else to describe what is happening.
