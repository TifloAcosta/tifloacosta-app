How to Use Two WhatsApp Numbers on the Same iPhone

Accessible written version of a Canal TifloAcosta video.

Introduction

Tony Acosta welcomes viewers to Canal TifloAcosta and introduces a WhatsApp feature that, as he explains in the video, had already appeared on Android and was being rolled out gradually to iPhone.

Before starting, he recommends checking the App Store to make sure WhatsApp is fully up to date and that no update is pending.

Tony recalls that, until then, using two WhatsApp numbers often meant using two different phones or relying on WhatsApp Business. The feature he demonstrates allows two accounts to be managed from the same iPhone, which can be useful, for example, when keeping a personal number separate from a work number.

Opening the account switcher

Tony opens WhatsApp on the iPhone and goes to the final tab in the bottom bar, labelled “You”.

Inside that tab, after his profile picture and the name Tony Acosta, he finds and activates the “Switch account” button.

WhatsApp then opens a panel titled “Accounts”. The main account is shown as selected, with the name Tony Acosta and the number +34 603 51 63 98.

Tony takes the opportunity to remind viewers that they can send a message to that number with their first name, surname, and place of residence to request membership of the TifloAcosta community.

Below the active account is the option “Add account”.

Adding a second account

Tony activates “Add account”. WhatsApp displays its welcome screen, including the terms of service and privacy policy.

From this point, Tony explains two different ways to continue, depending on what the user wants to achieve.

First option: move the number to this iPhone

The first possibility is to configure the second number directly as another account on this iPhone.

Tony explains that, if the user continues through “Agree and continue” and enters the new phone number, that number will then be used on this iPhone.

He warns that, if the number was previously operating as the main account on another phone, it will stop being used there in that way and will instead be configured on this iPhone alongside the first account.

Second option: link as an additional device

The second possibility is intended for keeping the account active on the other phone as well.

Tony explains that, in this case, the number should not be entered in the phone-number field. Instead, the user moves through the screen until reaching the “More” button and activates it.

From the menu that appears, he selects “Link as companion device”.

WhatsApp then displays a QR code on the iPhone screen. That code is scanned from the other device where the WhatsApp account is already active.

Tony explains that, once the devices are linked, chats and messages are synchronised and the same account can be used on both devices.

Switching between the two accounts

Once the second account has been added, Tony returns to the “Switch account” button in the “You” tab.

From that panel, the user can move between the main account and the second account, using each number independently from the same iPhone.

Conclusion

Tony finishes the demonstration by thanking everyone who supports Canal TifloAcosta. He also celebrates the channel having passed 4,000 subscribers and says goodbye to the audience.
