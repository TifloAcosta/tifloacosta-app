YouTube Finally Lets You Get Rid of Shorts

Accessible written version of a Canal TifloAcosta video.

Introduction

Tony Acosta demonstrates two features of the YouTube app on iPhone using VoiceOver. The first concerns controlling the amount of time spent in the Shorts feed. The second shows how to search for a YouTube channel and subscribe to it from within the app.

Opening time management

Tony explains that YouTube has begun introducing an option for limiting the amount of time spent in the Shorts feed each day.

To find it, he opens the “You” tab at the bottom-right of the app, announced by VoiceOver as tab 5 of 5.

Inside “You”, he moves to “Settings” and opens it. From the settings list, he selects “Time management”.

Usage information and daily limits

When “Time management” opens, YouTube shows information about time spent on the platform. Tony explains that the figures are based on the previous seven days and on watch history, excluding YouTube Music and YouTube TV.

As he moves through the screen, options such as “Remind me to take a break” and “Bedtime reminder” appear.

He then reaches the “Daily limits” heading.

Shorts feed limit

Under “Daily limits”, Tony finds “Shorts feed limit”. The description states that it controls how long the user can scroll through Shorts each day.

At that point in the demonstration, the setting is shown as disabled.

Tony activates the control to open the time selector. VoiceOver announces “Shorts feed limit 15 minutes”, which is the minimum value available in the demonstration.

What Tony explains about disabling Shorts

Tony makes clear that this feature is being rolled out progressively.

He explains that, when a 0-minute option becomes available, setting the limit to zero will prevent access to the Shorts feed. During the demonstration itself, however, the minimum value announced by VoiceOver is 15 minutes.

The same screen can also be used to choose other daily limits according to the user’s preference.

How to search for a YouTube channel

In the second part of the video, Tony explains how to find and subscribe to a channel.

He returns to the main screen by selecting the “Home” tab, announced as tab 1 of 5.

He activates “Search”, moves into the text field, types “tifloacosta”, and activates “Search” again to run the search.

Finding the channel in the results

Tony moves through the results until he reaches the relevant channel card.

During the demonstration, VoiceOver announces:

“tifloacosta @tifloacosta”.

It also reports “3.92K subscribers” and “272 videos”.

Tony continues moving forward with VoiceOver until he reaches the “Subscribe to Tiflo Acosta” button.

Subscribing to the channel

Tony activates “Subscribe to Tiflo Acosta” to complete the subscription.

He also reminds viewers that they must be signed in to YouTube with a Google account in order to subscribe to channels, like videos, or leave comments.

Conclusion

After demonstrating both features, Tony ends the explanation and says goodbye until the next Canal TifloAcosta video.
