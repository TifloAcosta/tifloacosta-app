# Android 1.3.0 — Actualidad chronology and retention addendum

Date: 2026-09-23

This addendum is part of the approved Android 1.3.0 design and overrides any earlier wording that could allow featured ranking to disturb the general Actualidad chronology.

## General Actualidad list

- The general Actualidad news list is strictly chronological by `publishedAt`, newest first.
- `featuredRank` must not move an older item above a newer item in the general list. Featured ranking may still be used in dedicated previews or portada-style selections where explicitly intended.
- The primary sort key is the publisher-provided publication date/time, not the time TifloAcosta discovered or ingested the item.
- Invalid or missing publication dates must not be allowed to masquerade as the newest content.

## Article metadata presentation

Each news item in the general list must expose, in an accessible reading order:

1. title;
2. source/publisher name;
3. publication date and time;
4. summary and existing actions.

The exact visual arrangement may vary, but screen-reader order must preserve that meaning.

`publishedAt` remains stored in a universal/ISO form. The displayed date and time must be formatted in the user's local device timezone and according to the selected app language.

## Retention

Actualidad is a rolling current-news catalog, not an unlimited archive.

- Keep at most 60 news items per language.
- Keep only items no older than 10 days from the reference generation time.
- Apply both rules: after excluding items older than 10 days, retain the 60 newest remaining items per language.
- When item 61 would enter a language catalog, the oldest retained item leaves that catalog.
- Expired/evicted items are no longer part of the current Actualidad feed, although other persistent resources or external source URLs are unaffected.

## New-item state

The Android 1.3.0 local `Nuevo`/new-count feature works only against the retained current catalog. Evicted or expired IDs must not reappear as new merely because the local baseline was pruned.

## Acceptance checks

Automated tests must prove:

- strict newest-first order even when an older item has `featuredRank`;
- source name plus localized date/time are rendered for a news card;
- a story older than 10 days is excluded from the generated mobile news catalog;
- a language catalog containing more than 60 eligible stories retains only its 60 newest;
- Spanish and English retention are capped independently;
- equal timestamps have a stable deterministic tie-breaker.
